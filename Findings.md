Key Insights & Findings
1. Geographic Distribution of Restaurants
Market Concentration: The analysis of restaurant counts per city shows that major metropolitan areas host the vast majority of listed establishments, reflecting high urban demand and dense delivery networks.

Top Cities: The top cities by listing volume highlight where market competition is most intense and where consumer engagement is highest.

2. Consumer Culinary Preferences
Most Popular Cuisines: Across the platform, specific categories (such as North Indian, South Indian, Fast Food, and Chinese/Beverages) dominate order trends and restaurant offerings, showing clear regional and mainstream food preferences.

3. Restaurant Chains vs. Independent Outlets
Branch Distribution: Identifying the restaurant chains with the highest number of branches reveals which brands have successfully scaled their franchise models across multiple cities, indicating strong brand loyalty and widespread market presence.

4. Ratings and Pricing Patterns
High-Rated Hubs: Filtering cities by average restaurant ratings (with minimum sample thresholds to ensure statistical significance) highlights the regions with the best overall dining and delivery experiences.

Cost vs. Quality: Analysis of pricing brackets in relation to ratings shows how different cost tiers perform in terms of customer satisfaction.

Recommendations
For New Entrants: Focus marketing and operational strategies on high-demand, underserved neighborhoods rather than saturated Tier-1 city centers.

For Menu Optimization: Prioritize top-performing cuisines in high-volume regions while tailoring offerings to local pricing preferences.
